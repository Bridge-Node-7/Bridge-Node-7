# Decision Surface

Bridge Node 7 should make consequential decisions easier to understand without hiding uncertainty or transferring unnecessary reconstruction work to the decision-maker.

A **Decision Surface** is the minimum human-facing projection of governed evidence, assurance state, dependencies, alternatives, and decision history required for an accountable person to understand what needs attention and act deliberately.

It is a presentation pattern, not a new system of record, portable contract, or decision authority.

## Purpose

The underlying estate may contain many sources, claims, assumptions, dependencies, receipts, experiments, contradictions, and historical records. The first decision view should expose only what materially changes the human choice.

The operating objective is:

> **Do the reconstruction, synthesis, and challenge before the accountable human arrives. Surface the smallest trustworthy decision view that preserves access to the evidence behind it.**

The useful result is less cognitive reconstruction around an important decision.

## Core surface

When supported by the governed record, a Decision Surface should answer:

1. **Mission** — what outcome this decision serves.
2. **Decision required** — the bounded choice or disposition.
3. **Why this reached you** — why this person owns or must review it.
4. **Why now** — the change, deadline, threshold, or new evidence creating materiality.
5. **What changed** — the delta from the previously reviewed state.
6. **Mission / capital / schedule effect** — the material consequence, when established.
7. **Prepared next move** — the bounded action or proof step supported by the current basis; this never replaces accountable human judgment.
8. **Strongest countercase** — the strongest material reason not to take the prepared move.
9. **Alternatives** — materially distinct options, including HOLD or NO ACTION when appropriate.
10. **Evidence posture** — direct support, applicability, freshness, contradictions, and material gaps without an invented confidence score.
11. **Critical unknowns** — unresolved facts that could change the decision.
12. **Minimum sufficient commitment** — the smallest reversible action that can create decision-relevant information when a full commitment is not yet justified.
13. **Cost of action / cost of waiting** — only when supported by evidence or explicit assumptions.
14. **Human burden** — expected internal attention or coordination load when material and supportable.
15. **Stop / reopen conditions** — what future evidence, changed dependency, or failed criterion should force reconsideration.
16. **Owner** — the accountable human decision owner.
17. **Need from you** — the smallest explicit human contribution required now.
18. **Show why** — governed evidence, assumptions, lineage, calculations, limitations, and historical state behind the surface.

Unsupported fields remain absent or explicitly unknown. They are never fabricated merely to complete the layout.

## Progressive disclosure

The same governed state may be rendered at different depths without creating competing truths.

### Signal

For executives or other time-constrained decision owners.

Target: understand the decision, why it matters, and the requested action in roughly one minute.

### Decision

For the accountable owner, technical lead, program lead, or reviewer.

Adds alternatives, evidence posture, unknowns, countercase, burden, and reopen conditions.

### Evidence

For engineers, scientists, analysts, counsel, security, procurement, or reviewers who need the complete support chain.

Includes exact provenance, assumptions, evidence envelopes, contradiction state, calculations, technical records, and historical lineage.

**One governed basis. Multiple projections. No duplicated authority.**

## Role-aware projection

Different people should not receive the same information density by default.

- **Executive / founder** — mission effect, why now, prepared move, capital/schedule exposure, major unknowns, and explicit ask.
- **Technical leader** — assumptions, dependencies, applicability, evidence gaps, reproduction state, changed conditions, and review trigger.
- **Scientist / engineer** — the specific claim, evidence, applicability boundary, contradiction, and smallest expert judgment required.
- **Operations / procurement** — supplier or operational condition, lead time, inventory or qualification state, mission dependency, and latest responsible action.
- **Legal / security** — classification, IP, access, handling, regulatory, disclosure, or security boundary relevant to the decision.

Role-aware presentation does not change the underlying evidence state or authority.

## Minimum necessary human contribution

Bridge Node 7 should continue without interrupting a person when the governed evidence is sufficient to do so safely.

When human input is required, ask for the smallest contribution that materially advances the decision.

Prefer:

> **We found X. Decision D depends on assumption A. We could not establish Y. Please mark: correct / incomplete / wrong.**

over:

> **Please reconstruct the case or fill out another assurance form.**

## Materiality and silence

A healthy assurance system does not create work merely because information changed.

Most changes should produce no interruption.

**NO ACTION REQUIRED** is a first-class disposition when no material decision basis changed.

Escalate only material novelty, contradiction, dependency change, handling change, evidence expiry, threshold crossing, or consequence.

The decision-maker's attention is a constrained mission resource.

## Countercase and uncertainty

A prepared next move should include the strongest material countercase when one is supported.

Do not express uncertainty as an opaque confidence percentage unless a defined probability model and evidence basis justify it.

Prefer inspectable posture such as:

- direct evidence state;
- applicability state;
- freshness;
- independent challenge or reproduction state;
- contradictions;
- critical unknowns;
- evidence gaps.

Confidence should come from orientation to the evidence and uncertainty, not manufactured precision.

## Reversible action under uncertainty

When evidence is incomplete, the preferred next move may be neither full commitment nor rejection.

Use the smallest action that can produce decision-relevant evidence while preserving acceptable optionality and avoiding unacceptable permanent downside.

Examples include a bounded experiment, a 30/60/90-day proof, a qualification run, a limited integration test, or a narrow information request.

This is a decision-preparation principle, not an automatic recommendation rule.

## Change-aware experience

For an existing decision, prefer a delta view over reconstruction of the full case.

A useful reassessment surface can show:

- assumptions changed;
- evidence strengthened or weakened;
- contradictions added or resolved;
- dependencies changed;
- reopen conditions triggered;
- decisions newly affected.

Historical decision-time state remains preserved.

## Burden discipline

Assurance should return more high-value human attention than it consumes.

When practical, measure:

- time to justified decision;
- executive or reviewer minutes per material decision;
- repeated evidence requests;
- decision reconstruction time;
- hidden assumptions discovered late;
- change-to-affected-decision identification time;
- review rework;
- integration surprises;
- decisions with explicit reopen conditions;
- outcome closure rate.

If the Decision Surface creates more coordination burden than decision value, simplify it.

## Authority boundary

A Decision Surface may organize, compress, compare, and explain governed information.

It does not:

- increase evidence certainty;
- certify scientific or technical truth;
- authorize deployment, release, acquisition, employment, investment, or publication;
- convert model output into canonical truth;
- replace qualified expert judgment;
- replace the accountable human decision owner.

The human still decides.

## Relationship to the BN7 estate

The Decision Surface composes existing authorities rather than creating another one.

- Intelligence Library preserves governed evidence, lineage, decision records, and outcomes.
- Domain systems produce bounded technical evidence.
- Frontier Mission Assurance preserves the reviewable decision basis and reopen conditions.
- Mission Graph prepares bounded dependency and change-impact context.
- Frontier Decision Engine prepares human-governed comparison and decision structure.
- Accountable humans make consequential decisions.
- Outcome records establish what actually happened.

A Decision Surface is the minimum-cognitive-load projection of that governed state for the person who must act.

## Permanent UX principle

> **The deeper the internal complexity, the simpler the human decision surface should become — without hiding uncertainty, provenance, or human authority.**
