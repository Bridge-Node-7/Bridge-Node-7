# Decision Lifecycle

Bridge Node 7 connects governed evidence to accountable decisions without turning analysis, automation, or transport into decision authority.

The lifecycle is organized around one practical question:

> **What is the supportable action now, why, and what evidence would require reconsideration?**

## Operating path

```text
decision question
      ↓
evidence acquisition and challenge
      ↓
governed intelligence
      ↓
domain assurance when material
      ↓
mission assurance when material
      ↓
dependency analysis when material
      ↓
decision surface
      ↓
accountable human decision
      ↓
decision receipt
      ↓
decision memory
      ↓
observed outcome
      ↓
reassessment
```

The path is compositional rather than mandatory. A decision uses only the systems needed to resolve its material uncertainty and preserve its decision basis.

## Operational use

The same lifecycle applies to internal and external work. The decision owner, evidence, handling boundary, and activated assurance systems may differ; the authority model does not.

For operational cases:

- begin with one bounded decision question, accountable owner, and evidence cutoff;
- keep case-specific non-public evidence and working records in the authorized working environment or governed private system;
- keep public repositories limited to reusable methods, portable contracts, synthetic examples, and material explicitly approved for public release;
- use Frontier Intelligence Workflows to structure research and produce a decision-ready brief when research is required;
- preserve the governed evidence basis and material unknowns before the accountable decision;
- activate domain assurance, Frontier Mission Assurance, or Mission Graph only when each materially improves the decision basis;
- present the Decision Surface with the supportable disposition or next move first;
- record the accountable human decision through Frontier Decision Engine when a durable Decision Receipt is required;
- move a supported Decision Receipt into Intelligence Library only as a review-required Decision candidate, never as automatic promotion;
- record the observed outcome when reality becomes observable, preserve the historical decision, and reassess when a reopen condition is triggered.

Internal use is not a lower-assurance mode. Customer, partner, program, and internal decisions use the same evidence, authority, handling, and outcome boundaries.

## Decision first

The first human-facing view should state the supportable disposition or next move before presenting supporting detail.

When the evidence supports a clear disposition, state it plainly. When evidence is insufficient for commitment, the first line should make that constraint equally clear.

Examples of ordinary-language dispositions include:

- proceed;
- proceed with conditions;
- experiment first;
- hold;
- do not proceed; and
- no action required.

These labels are presentation language, not a new machine authority or canonical decision state.

The [Decision Surface](DECISION_SURFACE.md) carries the concise view. Deeper evidence, provenance, assumptions, contradictions, calculations, and technical records remain available through progressive disclosure.

## Minimum sufficient commitment

When a full commitment is not justified, prepare the smallest reversible action that can materially reduce uncertainty.

A minimum sufficient commitment should identify, when supportable:

- the bounded next action;
- the decision hinge;
- the evidence expected from the action;
- time, capital, or coordination burden;
- a stop condition; and
- the evidence or event that should reopen the decision.

A smaller experiment is not automatically preferable. It is useful only when it can change the decision.

## Targeted activation

Each system retains one bounded role.

- **Frontier Intelligence Workflows** supports evidence-first collection, challenge, assessment, and decision-ready briefing.
- **Intelligence Library** preserves governed evidence, lineage, institutional decision memory, outcomes, and eligible learning.
- **Domain evidence systems** contribute specialized technical records when their domain is material to the decision.
- **Frontier Mission Assurance** preserves a reviewable assurance basis when claims, assumptions, experiments, applicability, reproduction, or reopen conditions require deeper assurance.
- **Mission Graph** prepares bounded dependency and change-impact context when dependencies materially affect the decision.
- **Frontier Decision Engine** prepares comparison and records an accountable human decision without becoming the decision authority.

Do not activate a system merely because it exists. Activate it when its authority materially reduces uncertainty, preserves a required boundary, or improves reconstructability.

## Authority boundary

Recommendation, preparation, validation, and decision are distinct.

A Bridge Node 7 assessment may state what the governed evidence supports. It does not become the accountable human decision.

A machine PASS establishes only the control that ran. It does not establish scientific truth, qualification, approval, deployment authority, investment authority, or any other consequential authorization.

A recorded human decision becomes institutional decision memory only through the governed review path of the system that owns that memory.

## Outcome boundary

Decision-time state and later reality remain separate.

An outcome record answers what was observed after the decision. It does not rewrite the evidence, rationale, or uncertainty that existed when the decision was made.

When outcomes or new evidence materially change the basis:

```text
preserve prior decision
      ↓
record observed outcome
      ↓
identify changed assumption / evidence / dependency
      ↓
reassess
      ↓
make a new decision when required
```

Reusable learning requires explicit governed promotion.

## Interoperability

Cross-system handoffs use explicit versioned contracts and exact integrity checks where a portable interface exists.

Compatibility follows the portable contract and its declared semantics, not incidental application versions.

Information transport must not:

- increase evidence certainty;
- erase contradictions or unknowns;
- convert preparation into approval;
- convert a recommendation into a human decision; or
- convert an observed outcome into a generalized learning claim.

Unsupported contract versions, broken integrity, missing authority, or unresolved handling boundaries fail closed.

## Completion standard

A decision lifecycle is complete when the applicable systems can preserve, without semantic loss:

1. the bounded decision question;
2. the governed evidence basis and material unknowns;
3. the concise Decision Surface;
4. the accountable human decision and receipt;
5. the reviewed institutional Decision Record;
6. the observed Outcome Record when reality becomes observable; and
7. the conditions for reassessment.

The objective is not maximum process. It is the smallest trustworthy path from evidence to accountable action and back to learning.
