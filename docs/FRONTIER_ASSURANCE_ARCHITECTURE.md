# Frontier Assurance Architecture

Bridge Node 7 develops **Frontier Assurance Infrastructure**: software and methods that connect technical evidence, engineering dependencies, and accountable decisions for frontier systems.

The purpose is practical: help teams understand what is supported, what remains uncertain, and what deserves another review when material conditions change.

## From evidence to decision

```text
Source evidence and technical records
                 ↓
    Engineering context and dependencies
                 ↓
       Reviewable decision basis
                 ↓
        Accountable human decision
                 ↓
     Targeted reassessment when needed
```

Evidence retains its source, stated limits, and uncertainty as it moves between methods and tools. Repetition is not independent corroboration; an automated PASS establishes only that the specified checks passed.

## Public reference capabilities

- **[Frontier Intelligence Workflows](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows):** trace claims to sources, distinguish independent evidence, and record open questions.
- **[Materials-to-Mission](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission):** relate selected materials, components, requirements, supplier information, and qualification gaps.
- **[Frontier Mission Assurance](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance):** connect assumptions, evidence, dependencies, and reviews to a decision basis that can be revisited as inputs change.
- **[Frontier Decision Engine](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine):** prepare human-governed decision briefs and bounded comparisons from supplied information.

Each repository identifies its own supported interfaces, validation scope, and licensing terms. The references demonstrate methods; they do not, by themselves, establish scientific validity, supplier or hardware qualification, certification, or operational authorization.

## Decision responsibility

The **Accountable human** retains consequential judgment. Software helps gather, trace, validate, compare, and present information without converting a hypothesis or simulated result into an established fact.

An unchanged decision basis may warrant **NO ACTION REQUIRED**. A meaningful change warrants reassessment of the affected evidence and decisions, not an automatic change in authority.

## Non-blocking assurance

- **Local changes remain local.** Validate work in the system responsible for it.
- **Cross-system verification is impact-driven.** Check affected interfaces and decision dependencies when a relevant boundary changes.
- **Estate observation is read-only and out of band.** It does not control ordinary engineering or release decisions.
- **Controls must remove more work than they create.** Prefer the smallest check that proves what changed.

## Repository-creation rule

Use a distinct repository only when it serves a distinct public purpose, user, rights boundary, or release lifecycle. Otherwise extend an existing reference without duplicating its responsibilities.
